# Q4 Negative Results

Negative results are model-selection evidence, not failed scratch work.

## W0 direct Q3 transfer

Q3-style handling does not solve Q4 because directional sources can disappear from visibility after being found. This motivates Q4-specific geometry and lifecycle modeling.

## W4-B continuity

Backbone continuity and insertion-style commitment over-constrain the rolling route. The result is higher movement and worse total time in the retained evidence, so the final method keeps W4-A style dynamic replanning.

## W6-A tail acceleration

Aggressive local acceleration reduces some extreme reacquisition tails, especially in stress cases. However, local improvements can change later completion timing and route ordering, producing severe same-seed regressions. It is useful mechanism evidence but not a final replacement.

## W7 macro-regret scheduling

The static macro-regret proxy does not reliably predict dynamic other-work regret in shadow calibration. The experiment was stopped before turning it into a formal policy benchmark.

## W8 stale-state repair

W8 improves some historical/stress tail indicators and remains a useful robustness comparison. It is not final because held-out 600 shows worse mean and pooled T/N than W5, plus many large paired regressions.

## Final selection rule

The final method must keep full clear while avoiding large hidden-distribution regressions. This conclusion applies to historical W6-A acceleration; the separately implemented W6-25PFR certificate is recorded in `results/q4/w6/feasible_region_full/`.
